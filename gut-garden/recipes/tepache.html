Fizzy Pineapple Tepache

Fermentation time: 1â€“3 days at room temperature; chill promptly when pleasantly tart

Recipe source: Ditch Soda for This Fizzy Pineapple Drink!

Ingredients

  • Peel and core from 1 ripe pineapple
  • 3/4 cup brown sugar or piloncillo
  • 6 cups filtered water
  • 1 cinnamon stick (optional)

Steps

  1. Dissolve sugar in water and add pineapple scraps.
  2. Cover loosely; ferment at room temperature until lightly tart and bubbly.
  3. Strain, bottle, refrigerate, and open carefully.

Safety note

Use a clean vessel. For a sealed second ferment, use pressure-rated bottles, check daily, refrigerate before opening, and discard if mold, rot, or a foul odor appears.
