Honey Ginger Chili Fermented Carrots

Fermentation time: Follow the source creator's two-stage method and timing

Recipe source: I Tried Fermenting Carrots and WOW!

Ingredients

  • Carrot sticks
  • 7% salt brine, as specified by the source creator
  • Fresh ginger
  • Fresh chili and lime
  • Fermented garlic honey, cloves included

Steps

  1. Pack the carrots beneath a 7% salt brine and allow the first fermentation to become active.
  2. After the first ferment, add fresh ginger, chili, lime, and fermented garlic honey.
  3. Mix, refrigerate, and serve when the carrots are bright, spicy-sweet, tangy, and still crisp.

Safety note

A 7% brine means 70 g salt per 1,000 g water. Keep carrots submerged, use clean equipment, and discard any batch with fuzzy mold, colored growth, slime, or a rotten odor. Refrigerate after flavoring.

Equipment

wide-mouth fermentation jar with weight/airlock
