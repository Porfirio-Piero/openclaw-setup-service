Ginger-Bug Fruit Soda

Fermentation time: 1â€“3 days bottled, depending on temperature and activity

Recipe source: Making Real Probiotic Soda With Ginger Bug

Ingredients

  • 1 quart cooled sweetened fruit tea or juice
  • 1/4 cup active ginger bug liquid
  • Flavoring such as citrus or ginger

Steps

  1. Combine cooled base with strained active ginger bug.
  2. Bottle in pressure-rated bottles and leave at room temperature.
  3. Check carbonation daily; refrigerate fully before opening.

Safety note

Carbonation can create dangerous pressure. Never use decorative bottles; inspect bottles, check daily, chill completely, and open outdoors away from faces.
