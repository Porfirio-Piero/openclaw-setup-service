Fermented Pears

Fermentation time: 2â€“5 days at room temperature

Recipe source: Fermented Pears: The Simple Trick

Ingredients

  • 4 firm pears, sliced
  • 4 cups filtered water
  • 2 tbsp non-iodized salt
  • Ginger or vanilla (optional)

Steps

  1. Make salt brine.
  2. Pack pears, cover completely, and secure with a fermentation weight.
  3. Taste daily and refrigerate while still pleasantly firm.

Safety note

Do not rely on wooden skewers as a sanitary weight. Fruit softens quickly; discard for mold, slime, or rotten odor.

Equipment
