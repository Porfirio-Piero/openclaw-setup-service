Fermented Grapes

Fermentation time: 2â€“4 days at room temperature

Recipe source: These Grapes Fight Back (Literally)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups seedless grapes
  • 2 cups filtered water
  • 2 tsp non-iodized salt
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • Ginger, coriander, and lemon peel

Steps

  1. Combine water, salt, and sugar.
  2. Add grapes and spices; keep fruit submerged.
  3. Ferment briefly and refrigerate before opening or serving.

Safety note

Fruit ferments may contain some alcohol and pressure. Keep away from children if alcohol is a concern; discard moldy or rotten batches.

Equipment

wide-mouth fermentation jar with weight/airlock
