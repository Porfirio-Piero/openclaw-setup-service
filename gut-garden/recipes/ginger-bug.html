Make a Ginger Bug First

Fermentation time: Usually 5â€“8 days to become active; refrigerated starter generally needs weekly feeding

Recipe source: How to Make a Ginger Bug — Wellness Mama

Ingredients

  • 2 cups filtered water
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped unpeeled organic ginger
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • Daily feed: 1 tbsp ginger plus 1 tbsp sugar

Steps

  1. Combine water, ginger, and sugar in a clean jar; cover with breathable cloth or a loose lid.
  2. Feed daily with ginger and sugar, stirring with a clean utensil.
  3. When reliably bubbly with a fresh gingery, lightly yeasty aroma, strain the needed liquid and continue feeding or refrigerate.

Safety note

Activity varies with temperature. Do not use a starter with fuzzy mold, colored growth, a rotten smell, or persistent inactivity. Carbonated drinks require pressure-rated bottles and daily pressure checks.

Equipment

wide-mouth starter jar plus pressure-rated bottles for finished soda
